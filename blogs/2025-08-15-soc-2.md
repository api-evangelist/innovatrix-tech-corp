What is SOC 2 Compliance? SOC 2 Compliance helps organizations meet the standards set by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA). SOC 2 measures how well a business manages customer data using five Trust Services Criteria (TSC): Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy.
