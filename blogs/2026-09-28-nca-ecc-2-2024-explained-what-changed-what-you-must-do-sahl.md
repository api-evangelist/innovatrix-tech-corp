What Is NCA ECC? (Quick Answer) The National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) issued NCA ECC (Essential Cybersecurity Controls) as Saudi Arabia’s cybersecurity baseline. The framework establishes cybersecurity controls designed to help organizations protect information and technology assets and manage cybersecurity risks.
