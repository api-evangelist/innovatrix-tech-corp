The Short Answer Sprinto is primarily designed around international compliance frameworks such as SOC 2 and ISO 27001. For organizations operating in Saudi Arabia, this can create a gap when compliance requirements extend to Saudi-specific frameworks such as PDPL and NCA ECC. This does not mean Sprinto is a poor compliance platform.
