The Short Answer OneTrust provides broad privacy and data governance capabilities that organizations can evaluate for Saudi PDPL compliance. However, support for a specific Saudi requirement depends on the product, configuration, and implementation approach. NCA ECC and SAMA CSF introduce additional cybersecurity control requirements that organizations must assess separately.
