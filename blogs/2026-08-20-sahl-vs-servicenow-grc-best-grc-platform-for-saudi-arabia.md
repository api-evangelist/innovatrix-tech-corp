Sahl and ServiceNow GRC both help organizations manage governance, risk, and compliance (GRC), but they take very different approaches. ServiceNow GRC operates within the broader ServiceNow enterprise workflow platform. It works particularly well for organizations that already use ServiceNow for IT service management, workflows, and enterprise operations.
